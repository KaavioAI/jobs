# Founding Account Executive

*See the [README](README.md) for an overview of Kaavio, how we work, who we're looking for, our interview process, and how to apply.*

Kaavio is winning enterprise distributors on the strength of a genuinely hard problem: turning messy product data into trustworthy content at scale. This is our **founding sales hire** — a full-time Account Executive who reports to the CEO and owns our sales motion with a lot of autonomy. You'll inherit a live pipeline of enterprise deals to ramp fast, run a real, blended methodology (Sandler, Challenger, MEDDPICC) with a seasoned sales advisor in your corner, and shape the go-to-market playbook the rest of the team is built on. Our model is **land and expand** — a focused first deal that grows several-fold as we prove outcomes — so you're not just closing logos, you're building accounts. Because we're defining a new category, you're often selling the vision as much as the product — and earning a technical buyer's trust in what our AI produces. This role is equal parts consultative selling, technical credibility, and process discipline.

### What you'll be doing

* **Owning a live enterprise pipeline end-to-end:** take over a book of in-flight deals — industrial, scientific, and electrical distributors evaluating Kaavio — and drive them to close with rigorous qualification, multi-threaded relationships, and mutual action plans.
* **Landing and expanding:** win a focused first deal, prove measurable outcomes, and grow the account several-fold. Expansion is where our revenue is — you own the whole arc, not just the first signature.
* **Selling a technical product credibly:** run demos and data-trust evaluations yourself. You'll get technical enough on our product and the distribution domain to earn a buyer's trust — and you'll set the evaluation criteria rather than let a buyer default to a feature checklist.
* **Qualifying with discipline:** confirm the economic buyer, map the decision and paper process, engineer forcing events, and disqualify early when the pain or budget isn't real. A clean, honest forecast is part of the job.
* **Running and sharpening our methodology:** we sell with a blended playbook (pain-first discovery, teaching the buyer a new frame, structured deal inspection). You'll run it, refine it by selling, and help make it repeatable for the next rep.
* **Generating your own pipeline:** an outbound motion that produces meetings, on top of founder-sourced and inbound deals.
* **Partnering with the founders on the hard deals:** co-selling the biggest opportunities early, then progressively owning them so the founders can step back into product and fundraising.
* **Feeding the product:** you're on the front line — what you hear in deals (and why you lose them) shapes what we build, and engineers will join your calls.

### Requirements

* A track record of **closing complex B2B deals** end-to-end — multi-stakeholder enterprise cycles and five-to-six-figure contracts — and **growing accounts after the first sale**.
* Comfort selling with **structured deal discipline**: you qualify hard (economic buyer, decision process, quantified pain), multi-thread, and forecast honestly. Familiarity with a MEDDIC/MEDDPICC-style approach is a strong plus.
* Ability to **sell a technical product to a technical-adjacent buyer**: you can learn a genuinely hard product and domain fast, run a data/technical evaluation, and hold your own on a substance-first call.
* Strong **discovery and consultative instincts**: you lead with the customer's outcome, teach the buyer a better way to think about their problem, and will walk from a deal that isn't a fit.
* Comfort operating with **autonomy and ambiguity** — you own your pipeline and take initiative without perfect specs or a fully built machine.
* **Strong writing skills:** clear outbound, crisp follow-ups, and notes that make a complex deal legible to the team.
* Must be legally authorized to work in the US. We are unable to provide sponsorship for this role.

### Nice to have

* **Entrepreneurial / startup experience** — you've founded or co-founded something, been an early employee, or been an early or first sales hire reporting directly to founders. You're comfortable building from scratch, wearing many hats, and creating the process rather than inheriting it (bonus if that included enterprise or international buyers). This is a big plus for us.
* Sold into **distribution, manufacturing, industrial, or scientific/lab** markets, or another catalog-heavy B2B space.
* Sold **data, AI, or developer/technical products** where the buyer needs to trust the output.
* Experience selling **PIM, ecommerce, ERP, or product-data** software, or to the teams that own that data.
* Ran a **land-and-expand** motion where initial deals were modest and grew on proven results.
* Comfortable being **hands-on technical** — running your own demos, reading a catalog, poking at the product.
